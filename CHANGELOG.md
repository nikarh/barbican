## [Unreleased]

### Features

- The nftables table and its sets, reconciled with what should be blocked
- The edge protocol: a journal of changes for edges, and their mirror of it
- Parsers for Traefik, sshd and Authelia logs, and for any log with a regular expression
- Rules, the ban policy and geolocation, with defaults for common attacks
- Storage, blocklists and the rule set, with parsers that borrow from the line instead of allocating
- The engine: records what rules matched, trusts addresses people log in from, and bans on thresholds
- Run the agent: log sources, the firewall, the edge API and the UI's RPC, with a systemd unit
- Barbican-edge: applies the agents' blocks on an OpenWrt router
- LuCI pages for barbican-edge: its status, and its settings in UCI
- The web UI: overview, addresses and what they did, events, bans, rules, lengths, countries, lists and blocklists
